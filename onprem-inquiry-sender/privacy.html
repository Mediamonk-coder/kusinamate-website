Privacy policy

This policy covers Onprem Inquiry Sender, the company’s website-inquiry storage and email-notification service. It does not replace the policies of separate shopping, payment or third-party services.

Current status: The inquiry backend exists. Gmail sending and the new website integrations have not been enabled. The sending behavior below describes how the prepared service works when enabled.

Information the service handles

  • Inquiry information: name, business name, email address or phone number, area of interest, optional message, source website, submission identifier and timestamps.
  • Notification records: the inquiry reference, configured recipient, processing status, attempt count and limited error codes. When Gmail sending is enabled, the service also records Gmail’s acceptance identifier and timestamp.
  • Sender connection: the designated sender’s verified Google email address and account identifier, OAuth client identifier, granted permissions, connection timestamps and an encrypted refresh credential.
  • Setup and abuse controls: short-lived authorization-session information, hashed session identifiers, and keyed hashes derived from contact details and a network-address signal. The application’s inquiry tables do not store the raw network address.

During authorization, Google provides identity and access tokens. The backend validates or uses these in memory; its database stores the encrypted refresh credential rather than the access or identity tokens. Hosting providers may maintain request or security logs separately.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to record the request, prevent duplicate or abusive submissions, notify the two configured internal company inquiry contacts, and support company follow-up. An inquiry email address may become the notification’s Reply-To address.

Google account information is used to verify that the connected account is the designated company-authorized Gmail sending account, maintain its authorization and send those notifications. The requested permissions are OpenID identity, email identity and Gmail send-only access. The service does not read existing mailbox messages.

Notifications use a fixed template. This service does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or provide it to AI models for training or inference. Its Google-data use is limited to the functions described here and is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and sharing

Supabase hosts the inquiry database and backend processing. Google processes authorization and, when sending is enabled, the notification emails. Inquiry contents are included in messages to the two configured internal company inquiry contacts, whose email services also process and store those messages. This is how the inquiry reaches the people handling it.

The refresh credential is encrypted before storage in a private database table. The encryption key and OAuth client secret are held in backend configuration. Public visitors cannot retrieve the connection or inquiry records through the service; access is restricted to backend services, project administrators and any explicitly authorized company staff.

The backend decrypts the refresh credential when it needs to renew access. These controls do not amount to a guarantee that data can never be accessed or lost. Provider logs, backups and mailbox copies are separate from the application’s database records.

Retention and deletion

The current implementation has no scheduled automatic deletion of inquiry records, notification history, sender-connection records or setup-session metadata. These records remain until an administrator removes them. No fixed retention period has been adopted for this service.

A setup authorization session expires after ten minutes. Its encrypted session payload is cleared when consumed, or when a later setup attempt processes expired sessions; the session’s metadata can remain. Expiration is not a promise that every record is deleted after ten minutes.

Abuse-control counters older than two days can be removed when the notification worker runs. A paused worker does not provide a fixed deletion deadline. Sent-mail and recipient-mailbox copies follow those accounts’ settings and are not deleted by this service.

Your choices and contact

The sender-account operator can remove the app’s access through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future token renewal; an already issued access token may remain usable until Google invalidates it or it expires. Contact the company to pause sending immediately and request removal of stored connection data. Removing Google access does not itself delete existing inquiry or email records.

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by this service, contact admin@opcoutletshop.com. Include enough information to identify the inquiry or sender connection, but do not email passwords, setup secrets or access tokens. Requests require operator review; there is no self-service deletion screen.
