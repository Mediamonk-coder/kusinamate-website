Terms of use

These terms concern the Onprem Inquiry Sender service operated for Onprem Concepts Corp: recording website inquiries and sending company notifications when enabled.

Current status: Setup is in progress. Gmail sending is disabled.

Permitted use

Use inquiry forms for genuine business or product inquiries and provide contact details you are authorized to share. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, access tokens, unsolicited bulk messages or content intended to disrupt the service.

Only an authorized company operator may connect the designated sender account, patsappbot@gmail.com. The operator must control that account and review Google’s requested permissions. Visitors do not need to connect a Google account.

What a notification means

A confirmed save means that an inquiry was stored. It does not confirm email delivery, that a staff member has read it, or a response time. Gmail’s acceptance of a message is also not proof of delivery or reading. Notifications can be delayed, rejected, paused or require operator review.

The service records and forwards inquiries. It does not place orders, take payments, confirm prices, approve franchises or manufacture products. The company handles those matters separately.

Connection and service controls

The company operator controls whether sending is enabled and may pause the service for setup, maintenance or investigation. The sender account’s Google authorization can be revoked through Google Account connections. Contact support to request an immediate sending pause.

Disconnecting Google access does not remove stored inquiries or previously sent messages. Storage, retention and deletion requests are described in the privacy policy.

Support

Contact admin@opcoutletshop.com about this service, its Google connection, a saved inquiry or a data request.
