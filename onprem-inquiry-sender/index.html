Website inquiries, routed to the company team.

Onprem Inquiry Sender is a company tool that stores website inquiries and, when sending is enabled, emails a notification to the two configured company inquiry contacts.

Current status: Setup is in progress. Gmail sending is disabled.

What the service does

It records the visitor’s name, business name, contact details, area of interest, optional message and source website. The company uses this information to review and follow up on the inquiry.

Notifications are sent from patsappbot@gmail.com with the display name “Anna, AI assistant to Patrick Ong.” They use a fixed message template; this service does not send inquiry or Google account data to an AI model.

Who connects a Google account

Only the designated company operator connects the sender account. Website visitors do not sign in to Google or grant Gmail access to submit an inquiry.

The operator’s Google authorization identifies the sender account and permits sending email. The service does not request permission to read, search, modify or delete existing Gmail messages, or to access Google contacts, calendars or Drive files.

After the operator authorizes access, an encrypted refresh credential allows the backend to renew its sending authorization without repeated sign-in. The operator can revoke the connection through their Google Account.
